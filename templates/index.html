Hull Cleaning

Scheduled Maintenance

Monthly & Bi-Monthly Hull Cleanings

It’s advisable to clean and inspect your boat’s bottom at least once per month. In areas with rapid marine growth, especially during the summer, biweekly cleanings may be beneficial. Extending the time between cleanings can reduce efficiency, accelerate wear on bottom paint, and increase overall cleaning costs. Significant fouling on running gear may also cause vibrations that could lead to vessel damage.

Hull Cleanings Include:

  • Hull Inspection
  • Thorough Cleaning of Hull, Running Gear, and Thru-Hulls
  • Anode Assessment
  • Anode Replacement
  • A Detailed Boat Report
  • Photos/Video

Propellers

Boat propellers can sustain damage from groundings, impacts with debris, or electrolysis and galvanic corrosion caused by electrical faults onboard or nearby, as well as neglected anodes.

Even minor propeller damage can generate vibrations that may go unnoticed during operation but still place stress on components such as cutlass bearings, couplings, and the transmission. Promptly addressing any prop damage is recommended to help avoid more extensive and costly repairs over time.

Propeller services we provide:

  • Underwater removal and installation various types of propellers
  • Prop damage assessment
  • Propspeed and antifouling coatings
  • Photos/Video

Rudders

Importance of maintaining your rudder:

  • Regularly inspect your rudder for damage, cracks, or loose fittings.
  • Ensure the rudder is properly aligned for optimal performance and to prevent excessive wear.
  • Our divers will inspect your rudders to identify and address any potential issues each time they clean your boat.

We can oftentimes repair, or replace, bent or broken rudders without having to haul your boat out.
